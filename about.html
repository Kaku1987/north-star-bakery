Our Story

North Star Bakery is a local bakery dedicated to producing fresh baked goods for local community.

Our Sourcing Philosophy

We are a product-focused business. Our main products are bread, pastries and cakes. We pride ourselves on our handmade products and the use of fresh, high-quality ingredients. You are welcome to visit our shop to browse and purchase. For group orders, please telephone us for further details.

Our Team

We are a small team of five, but each of us has extensive baking experience and is committed to providing fresh, delicious bread and pastries.
